About Kilnfield

Kilnfield brings together everyday interface symbols and illustrations of the world around them, from transport and energy to weather and the environment. The icons use a shallow three-quarter view, rounded edges and muted clay colours.

File formats

Every icon comes as a transparent 256 × 256 PNG and a lossless WebP. The SVG embeds the PNG in a single self-contained file. It preserves the original lighting and colours, and the artwork inside it remains a bitmap at its original resolution.

Search

Search by name, appearance or purpose. Kilnfield ranks matches immediately using icon descriptions and related words, then uses a small language model when available. The model runs in your browser, so search phrases stay on your device.
