About Scout Icons

The collection started with the illustrations Scout uses to depict infrastructure, technology, public issues and environmental events. It adds everyday interface symbols in the same softly sculpted style, with a shallow three-quarter view, rounded edges and muted clay colours.

File formats

Every icon comes as a transparent 256 × 256 PNG and a lossless WebP. The SVG embeds the PNG in a single self-contained file. It preserves the original lighting and colours, and the artwork inside it remains a bitmap at its original resolution.

Search

Search by name, appearance or purpose. Scout ranks matches immediately using icon descriptions and related words, then uses a small language model when available. The model runs in your browser, so search phrases stay on your device.

Licensing

The source artwork came with no general reuse license, and the public catalog grants no additional reuse rights. National flag illustrations are stylized depictions, and their use may be subject to national-symbol rules. See the repository for provenance and coverage details.
